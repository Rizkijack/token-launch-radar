The value model

The board is a claim about what a launch would be worth if a protocol's record stayed as it is. It states the rule openly so a reader can check it against the snapshot.

THE FITTED RELATIONSHIP
mcap = 10^a x (TVL / $100M)^b x 10^(growth + overdue)
The curve is fitted on the protocols that already record a token. Their TVL and market cap are facts in the snapshot. A pre-token protocol is priced by placing its TVL on that line, then shifting by its seven-day TVL change and by the time it has run with no recorded token. No protocol name enters the calculation.

HOW TO READ THE OUTPUT
P10 and P90 come from the spread of the fitted line. They bound a range of estimates, not a forecast. Net if launch applies an explicit assumed share of the implied value to a stated position, then subtracts the position. Net per hour divides that by the hours the farm plan takes. The verdict thresholds are stated above and applied uniformly to every row.
The snapshot carries TVL, seven-day change, market cap and the day DefiLlama began tracking a protocol. It carries no fee or active-account figures, so none are shown. An on-chain action is never a guarantee of a reward.
